About us

Booking a massage should be as relaxing as the massage itself.

Why we built BookMySpa

Most people still book a spa appointment the same way they did twenty years ago — a phone call during business hours, hoping someone picks up, hoping the slot you want is actually free. Spa owners, meanwhile, lose bookings to missed calls and no-shows they never see coming. BookMySpa exists to fix both sides of that problem at once.

We're a booking platform for massage and spa services — browse real spas near you, see live availability down to the room, pay securely (or reserve now and pay at the counter, your choice), and get a confirmation you can actually rely on.

What spa owners get

A dashboard built around how a real spa actually runs: define your rooms and their capacity so you never get double-booked, run time-limited promotions, and see every booking — and every rupee owed — in one place, whether it was paid online or will be settled at the counter.

Our approach

We'd rather get a handful of neighborhoods genuinely right than spread thin across a whole country from day one. If you don't see your area listed yet, that's next — not never.
