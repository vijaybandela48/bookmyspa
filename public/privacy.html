1. Who we are

SpaBook ("we", "us", "our") operates a booking platform connecting customers with independent spa and massage businesses ("Partner Spas"). This policy explains what information we collect through our website and app, why, and what choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Account information: name, email address, and phone number when you register. Your phone number is verified by a one-time code (OTP) before your account is created.

Booking information: the spa, service, date/time, and amount for each booking you make, and any coupon codes used.

Payment information: when you pay online, your payment is processed directly by our payment partner (Razorpay); we receive confirmation that payment succeeded and a transaction reference, but we never receive or store your full card number, CVV, or UPI PIN. When you choose to pay at the spa, we record the amount due and, once collected, how it was paid (cash, card, or UPI) — recorded by the spa, not processed by us.

Location information: if you use the "Nearby" search feature, we request your device's location with your permission, to sort spas by distance. We do not store this location after your session.

Communications: if you message us through the Contact page, we keep your name, email, and message to respond to you.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and verify your phone number
  • To process and confirm your bookings, and send confirmations by email, SMS, or WhatsApp (your choice)
  • To let Partner Spas see the booking details needed to serve you (your name, contact number, and what you booked)
  • To respond to support requests
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access (for example, limiting repeated login or verification-code attempts)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your booking history to show you third-party advertising.

4. Who we share information with

Partner Spas: when you book, the spa you booked with receives your name, phone number, and booking details — they need this to prepare for your visit and reach you if needed.

Service providers: we use third-party providers to operate the platform, who process data on our behalf: a payment processor (Razorpay) for online payments, and messaging providers for email/SMS/WhatsApp delivery. These providers are only given the information they need to perform their specific function.

Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of SpaBook, our users, or the public.

5. Data retention

We keep account and booking information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward for accounting, dispute resolution, and legal compliance purposes. You can request deletion of your account as described below.

6. Your rights and choices

  • You can view and update your account details by logging in
  • You can cancel a booking that hasn't yet completed, subject to the cancellation terms shown at the time of booking
  • You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or request that we delete your account, by contacting us at the details below
  • You can choose your preferred booking-confirmation channel (email, SMS, or WhatsApp) at checkout

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted password storage, rate-limiting on login and verification attempts, and secure signature verification on payments. No system is perfectly secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

8. Children's privacy

SpaBook is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect information from, children under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and post material changes prominently.
